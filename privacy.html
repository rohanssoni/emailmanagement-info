Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

emailmanagement is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own Gmail accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

It uses Google's view-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly) to read email counts, message details such as sender, subject, date, and size, and, when needed, the text of individual emails. It cannot change, delete, or send email.

What it stores

On the owner's own computer, it stores sign-in tokens for each connected account and daily email counts. It does not store email content, and it runs no servers of its own.

Who it shares data with

When the owner asks Claude to review their email, the details Claude retrieves are sent to Anthropic, the provider of Claude, so that Claude can process the request. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
